Character Profile

Ahbangkun is a 3D Blender artist from Sarawak, Malaysia. Most of the work is tokusatsu: transformation belts, devices, weapons and heroes from Kamen Rider, Ultraman and Super Sentai, plus anime and manga characters from series like Gundam, One Piece, Kaiju No. 8, Kagurabachi and Ichi the Witch.

On TikTok (65.8K followers, 1.7M likes) the most-watched video passes 1.1M views. The 3D models live on Sketchfab, most of them free to download. Commissions are open, including low poly 3D models. Ahbangkun also makes comics, with three WEBTOON Canvas series: “I'm a good guy.”, “Isekai Is Amazing!!” and “I Was Reincarnated As a Demon”.

Loves 3D modeling, drawing, and 2D and 3D animation. Also codes a little (basic), and is learning Japanese as a beginner. 頑張ります!
